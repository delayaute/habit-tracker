youtube-uploader - Privacy Policy

youtube-uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sven Mouton, to upload and schedule videos on his own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, change their visibility and read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own channel.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
